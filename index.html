Background

Psychology meets technology.

My career started with a Master's in Counseling and a foundation in Psychology, and that baseline shapes how I approach systems today. I understand how people resist change, how adults assimilate new software, and how cognitive load dictates tech adoption. In AI and digital transformations, those are not soft skills, they are the exact dividing line between a platform people use and one they abandon.

Most recently, I have served as Webmaster and UX Product Owner for the State of Indiana (DWD), Multimedia Design Manager at RJL Solutions leading 20+ client platforms, and an accessibility advocate on state-level user councils. Whether coordinating high-stakes website migrations, conducting 38-participant UX research studies, or coding Python automations, my mission remains constant: tease out clarity from complexity.

I focus on AI adoption and AI assisted and multimodal interfaces. The computational tools are here; the human side of the transition is what needs intentional design.

Meta Case Study

I built this site with AI.

Not as a gimmick. As a live demonstration of how human discernment directs AI execution.

This site was built using a collaborative workflow with Claude for structural drafting, content strategy, and component logic, and Gemini for WCAG contrast auditing and accessibility validation. Every section was refined through conversation, not produced in an unreviewed single prompt.

That distinction is crucial. Genuine AI fluency is not knowing how to write prompts; it is knowing when an output fails accessibility guidelines, introduces cognitive friction, or needs human pushback.

  1. 1 Planning with Claude: Outlined portfolio objectives, persona journeys, and semantic structure without reaching for off-the-shelf templates.
  2. 2 Accessibility validation with Gemini: Generated and stress-tested the mathematical color contrast matrix below, verifying WCAG 2.2 AA compliance before locking colors.
  3. 3 Iterative code refinement: Generated CSS and semantic HTML with dark mode, disclosure ARIA states, and keyboard traps resolved upfront.
  4. 4 Human judgment at each gate: Curating narrative focus, maintaining brand restraint, and testing against real-world browser screen readers.
