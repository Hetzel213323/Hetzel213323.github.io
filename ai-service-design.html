From Napkin Sketch to Governed Architecture

Complex service design never begins with automated code or black-box LLM prompts. It starts by untangling stakeholder friction, data flows, and accountability boundaries on paper.

01 • The Ideation Artifact

Initial Stakeholder Logic

Initial napkin architecture sketched during a strategic workforce systems consultation. It mapped the fundamental instinct: filter the 1,000-candidate pool into top candidates and route them through human approval before any outreach occurs.

02 • The Governed Blueprint

Production Service Architecture

The engineered service blueprint. We introduced demographic tensor stripping to prevent bias, confidence threshold routing (<85% triggers mandatory review), and auditable state reporting milestones.

The Design Principle: Technology should never outpace governance. By identifying the human review gate during the earliest conceptual phase, we prevented the catastrophic hallucination and disparate impact risks that plague fully autonomous hiring tools.
Phase 01 Cognitive Load Diagnosis

Phase 1: Administrative Bottlenecks

Workforce development specialists and public agency case managers are overwhelmed by clerical overhead. When hundreds of citizen resumes flow into state portals alongside incoming employer job orders, staff spend up to 70% of their workday opening PDFs, manually copying information, and cross-referencing eligibility rules.

This is not an issue of human speed; it is an issue of administrative friction. Every hour spent manually filtering spreadsheets is an hour stolen from personalized interview prep, barrier removal, and supportive counseling for citizens undergoing high-stress career transitions.

The Friction Point

Keyword matching algorithms often disqualify candidates with non-linear career paths or transferable skills.

The Human-Centered Goal

Automate unstructured document parsing and skill vectorization so humans focus entirely on advising.

Phase 02 Human Judgment: Bias Oversight & Audit

Phase 2: Intake & Semantic Matching

When an employer submits a requisition, the semantic ingestion pipeline vectorizes the core competencies required, rather than searching for exact keyword strings. The agent searches the regional citizen pool, evaluating transferable skill vectors alongside state grant eligibility (e.g., Indiana Workforce Ready Grant).

Bias Shielding: During initial triage, demographic markers (graduation years, residential zip codes, names) are stripped from ranking tensors. Edge cases scoring below 85% confidence are flagged for manual review rather than automatically rejected.

Transferable Skill Mapping

Recognizes that retail inventory management translates directly into advanced manufacturing logistics.

Explainable Matching

Generates plain-language rationale for every match, preventing opaque black-box decisions.

Phase 03 Calibrated Batching

Phase 3: Specialist Review Cockpit

Rather than overwhelming workforce specialists with an unranked pool of 1,000 applicants or arbitrary 100-row spreadsheets, the triage engine filters the cohort down to a calibrated, high-probability tier (typically 4 to 8 candidates at a time). In this working demonstration, we surface a focused cohort of four to illustrate the human review gate.

The career professional retains total agency: choosing who moves forward, editing communication tone, and triggering personalized outreach with explicit human consent.

Phase 04 Human Judgment: Final Approval & Voice Check

Phase 4: Outreach & Tone Calibration

Automated cold emails feel robotic and generate anxiety. In TalentBridge, outreach emails are drafted in the voice of the assigned career specialist, referencing specific transferable strengths that made the candidate stand out.

The Safeguard: The system cannot dispatch emails autonomously. The specialist must review, edit nuances if needed, and give explicit consent. This keeps human empathy and genuine professional guidance at the heart of the interaction.

Tone Normalization

Plain, supportive language calibrated to reduce the anxiety of job transitions.

Full Audit Trail

Every approved draft and human edit is logged for compliance and continuous alignment.

Phase 05 Grounded RAG Assistant

Phase 5: Citizen Agent & Logistics FAQ

Once outreach is sent, citizens often hesitate because they need fast clarity on practical logistics: pay rate, hybrid flexibility, bus line accessibility, or training prerequisites.

Instead of forcing a case manager to answer dozens of repetitive logistics emails each day, a grounded retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) assistant answers verified job questions instantly, 24/7, across SMS or web.

  • Candidate Inquiry

    "Hi! I received the outreach email for the QA Tech role. Does this company support the Workforce Ready Grant for the ISO certification, and is there public transit nearby?"

  • AI Logistics Assistant

    "Hello Elena! Yes, this employer partners with the Indiana Workforce Ready Grant to cover 100% of the ISO 9001 prep training tuition. They are located along transit lines with a direct stop at the facility. Would you like me to book a 15-minute phone prep call with your counselor, Matt?"

Phase 06 Employer Feedback Loop

Phase 6: Outcomes & Continuous Learning

When candidates opt in, the specialist conducts live 1-on-1 interviews, focusing on motivational alignment and cultural fit. AI assists solely by organizing interview notes, drafting employer briefing summaries, and logging state reporting milestones.

Continuous Learning: If an employer notes that candidates lacked a particular safety certification, that feedback updates the semantic vector weights for the next requisition, ensuring the matching engine continually sharpens over time.

Product Discernment • Google Flow Session

Knowing When to Reject Superficial AI Gloss

During visual exploration in Google Flow, I prompted the model to explore multiple architectural treatments from my raw napkin sketch: a digital whiteboard, a minimal wireframe, and a high-gloss 3D enterprise visual.

While generative models love generating glowing 3D chips and holographic dashboards, true product design requires discernment. Glossy marketing graphics obscure real-world logic. By rejecting the superficial 3D gloss in favor of the clean, structured wireframe, we kept the system transparent, auditable, and grounded in actual public sector constraints.
